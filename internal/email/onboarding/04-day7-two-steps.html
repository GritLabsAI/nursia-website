Dosage questions

Dosage questions are usually two steps, not one.

The wrong answers are what you get from one misplaced decimal, so the setup matters more than the arithmetic. Try it before you scroll.

PH-212 · Pharmacological therapies · Single answer

The provider prescribes heparin 25,000 units in 250 mL of 0.9% sodium chloride to infuse at 1,000 units/hour. At what rate in mL/hour should the nurse set the pump?

A
4 mL/hour
B
10 mL/hour
C
25 mL/hour
D
40 mL/hour

Answer

B
10 mL/hour

Find the concentration first, then divide the ordered dose by it: 25,000 units ÷ 250 mL = 100 units/mL
1,000 units/hour ÷ 100 units/mL = 10 mL/hour The other options are the answers a single slipped decimal gives you, which is exactly what the item is testing.
