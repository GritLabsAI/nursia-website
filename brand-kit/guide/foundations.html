Foundations · 01

Colour, type, space

Eleven colours, three faces, one spacing rhythm. The system is small on purpose — there is no variant you can reach for to get out of a hard layout problem.

Colour

The ground

Paper is the default background of every surface, in the product and in the marketing. Pure white is reserved for cards, which is what makes a card read as an object lying on a page rather than a rectangle drawn on one.

The pale red behind a wrong row.

never adjacentteal + highlight

Teal and highlighter never touch. Put paper or ink between them.

Colour rules, in full. No gradients. No drop shadows except the card's. No glow. No third accent. Green and red are states — they may never be used as decoration, and a correct answer is the only thing on the site allowed to be green.

Space

A 4px grid and a lot of restraint

Every measurement is a multiple of 4. The site runs on Tailwind's default scale, which is the same thing said differently. What matters more than the numbers is the ratio: the gap between two bands should be larger than anything inside either of them, so the page reads as a stack of ideas rather than a wall.

Motion

Motion exists to confirm something happened, never to entertain. Three durations, one easing curve, and full respect for prefers-reduced-motion.

MoveTimingWhere
State change120–160ms easeHover and press on buttons, rows, keys.

opacity + 4px rise		A rationale appearing after an answer is submitted.
Highlighter swipe620ms cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.3,1)The stroke drawing itself in, once, on the hero only.
Nothing loops. Nothing parallaxes. Nothing animates on scroll more than once. If motion would still be running while someone is trying to read a rationale, it is wrong.

Accessibility

The floor, not the aspiration

  • Contrast. Measured against paper #FBFAF6: ink 17.3:1, ink-2 10.4:1, teal 6.1:1, wrong 5.7:1, muted 5.1:1, correct 4.8:1. White on teal is 6.4:1, paper on ink 17.3:1, ink on the highlighter 14.3:1. Everything clears AA; muted and correct are the two that sit closest to the line, so neither is ever used below 11px or for body copy.
  • Never colour alone. A correct row carries a border, a fill and a coloured key. Red and green are reinforced by position and wording so a colour-blind user loses nothing.
  • Focus is visible and teal. 2px outline, 2px offset, 2px radius. It is never removed.
  • 44px targets everywhere, including answer rows, which are buttons.
  • Reduced motion cuts everything to 0.001ms and disables smooth scrolling.
