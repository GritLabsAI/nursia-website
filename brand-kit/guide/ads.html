Ad creative

The same system at 1080×1350. One idea, one headline, one supporting line, one CTA. The offer in every ad is the free tier — fifty questions, no card, full rationales — and nothing else.

Rules

What holds across every ad

ElementRule
Format1080×1350 (4:5) by default. 1080×1080 and 1080×1920 are cut from the same layout, never redesigned.
GroundPaper #FBFAF6, or the ink ground for one concept in a set. Never white.
FillUnderfilled. If the layout feels comfortable, remove something.
AlignmentLeft, always. One optional centred concept per campaign, maximum.
HighlighterOne stroke, behind one phrase, in one ad out of a set — not in every one.
LogoSmall, bottom-left. Ink cut on paper, reverse on ink, white on photography. It is never the hero.
CTAA teal rectangle, 3px radius, white display text at 600, about 44px tall. On ink it is highlighter yellow with ink text.
SeparatorsHairlines. Never a box inside a box.
OfferThe free tier only. Never a price, an upgrade, or a paid feature.

Compliance

What gets an ad rejected

  • Copy that asserts something about the viewer. "Failed the NCLEX?" is a Meta rejection. Reframe as a fact about a population.
  • A pass rate or score claim, in the image or the caption.
  • A fabricated testimonial or user count.
  • Anything implying NCSBN endorsement, including using the NCLEX logo or an official-looking seal.
  • Before/after framing of a person's performance or circumstance.
