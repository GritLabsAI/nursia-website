Layout patterns

A page is a stack of bands inside a 1140px container. Each band holds one idea, opens with a mono eyebrow, and is separated from the next by space rather than by a box.

Anatomy

How a page is built

BandGroundWhat it does
HeaderpaperWordmark left, two or three links, one teal button. A hairline under it, never a shadow.
HeropaperEyebrow, one display headline with the single highlighter stroke, one serif line, one button. Often a question card beside or below it.
Proofink + flowsheet gridThe stat strip. One dark band per page, and this is usually it.
ExplainpaperTwo or three bands of serif prose at a 42rem measure, each with its own eyebrow and head.
Indexpaper-2A grid of cells — topics, item types, guides. Quiet, scannable, no illustration.
QuestionspaperThe accordion. Native details, crawlable, no more than eight.
Closeink or photoOne line, one button, the reverse or white wordmark.
FooterpaperMono links in columns, fine print, the legal notice that Nursia is not affiliated with the NCSBN.
One dark band per page. The ink ground is a punctuation mark. Two of them and the page stops feeling like paper.

Breakpoints

Responsive behaviour

WidthContainerWhat changes
< 560px100% − 48pxEvery grid collapses to one column. Hero card moves below the headline. Band padding drops to 48px.
560–900px100% − 64pxFour-up grids become two-up. The split rule stacks: mono block above, serif below.
900–1140px100% − 64pxFull layout, container fluid.
> 1140px1140px fixedThe container stops growing. Bands still run full-bleed; only their contents are capped.
Type does not scale down much — the hero uses clamp(38px, 5vw, 56px) and body copy stays at 17px on a phone. People read rationales on phones; shrinking the serif is the one change that would actually hurt.

Things that are never on a page

  • A carousel of testimonials, or any testimonial that was not actually said.
  • A countdown, a "only 3 spots left", or any urgency device.
  • A pass-rate figure, a score claim, or a number of users.
  • An illustration of a cartoon nurse. There are no illustrations at all.
  • Two accent colours, two dark bands, or a shadow on anything that is not the card.
  • A modal that interrupts someone mid-question.
